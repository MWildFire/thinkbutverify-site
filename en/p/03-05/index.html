Room 03 · Who is watching?

Case 03/05. Cable ↔ metadata Cable ↔ metadataCable ↔ metadataCable ↔ metadata

Кабель ↔ метаданные

Art

Artist
Trevor Paglen; Evan Roth; Simon Weckert
Work
Undersea cables; Internet Landscapes; Google Maps Hacks
Year
2015; since 2016; 2020

Paglen photographs tapped undersea cables, Roth the shores where they land. Weckert pulled 99 phones in a cart and Google Maps showed a traffic jam.

↔

Code

metadata

The internet is physical, and your phone keeps broadcasting: network names, coordinates, time. Photo metadata records where and when it was taken.

Action

Remove the location tag from a photo before posting it.

Удалите геометку из фото перед публикацией.

The label in the room

The same hash is printed at the foot of the label. If they match, nobody changed the text.

The internet is physical: cables, shores, 99 phones in a cart that fooled a map. Your phone keeps broadcasting too. Remove the location tag from a photo before posting it.

Интернет физический: кабели, берега, 99 телефонов в тележке, которые обманули карту. Ваш телефон тоже всё время излучает: сети, координаты, время. Удалите геометку из фото перед публикацией.
