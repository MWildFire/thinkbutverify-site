After the FBI questioned him in 2002, Elahi began publishing his every move himself: meals, rooms, coordinates. Self-surveillance became an alibi.

digital footprint

Every day your phone records where you were, who you wrote to and what you photographed. Some of it goes to apps you forgot about.

Action

Open settings and revoke location access from apps that do not need it.
