Не трубка ↔ не банк

Art

Artist
René Magritte
Work
The Treachery of Images
Year
1929
Where
Los Angeles County Museum of Art (LACMA)

Under a painted pipe Magritte wrote “Ceci n'est pas une pipe” — “This is not a pipe”. He is right: it is a picture of a pipe.
