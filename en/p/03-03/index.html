Case 03/03. NINA ↔ watermark NINA ↔ watermarkNINA ↔ watermarkNINA ↔ watermark

NINA ↔ водяной знак

Art

Artist
Al Hirschfeld; Dmitri Shostakovich
Work
The name NINA in the drawings; the D–S–C–H motif
Year
since 1945; 1960

Hirschfeld hid his daughter's name NINA in every drawing. Shostakovich encoded himself in notes: D–E♭–C–B spells D. Sch. in German notation.

↔

Code

watermarks

Files are signed secretly too: an invisible watermark in a photo, a mark in a document that reveals who leaked a copy.

Action

Play the D–E♭–C–B motif on the keyboard.

Сыграйте мотив D–Es–C–H на клавиатуре.

The label in the room

The same hash is printed at the foot of the label. If they match, nobody changed the text.

Hirschfeld hid the name NINA in his drawings, Shostakovich his initials in notes. Files are signed the same way, with an invisible watermark. Play D–E♭–C–B on the keyboard.

Хиршфельд прятал в рисунках имя NINA, Шостакович — свои инициалы в нотах. Файлы подписывают так же: невидимым водяным знаком. Сыграйте мотив D–Es–C–H на клавиатуре.
