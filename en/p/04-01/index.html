1 % беспорядка ↔ лава-лампа

Art

Artist
Vera Molnár
Work
1 % of disorder (Un pour cent de désordre)
Year
1970s

Molnár built strict grids and added one percent of chance. Without it the pattern is dead; with it, alive.

Code

random numbers

Encryption keys are strong only if no one can guess them. Cloudflare films a wall of about a hundred lava lamps and turns the frames into random numbers.

Action
