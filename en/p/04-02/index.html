Work
Malware Museum; The Persistence of Chaos
Year

The Malware Museum on archive.org runs neutralised DOS viruses: Casino, Ambulance, Cascade. In 2019 a laptop carrying six viruses sold as an artwork for $1.345 million.

↔

Code

malware

A virus copies itself into other files. Old viruses showed pictures; today's silently encrypt your data and demand a ransom.
