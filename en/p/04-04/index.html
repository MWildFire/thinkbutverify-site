Тетрис ↔ хакер

Art

Artist
Chaos Computer Club; Alexey Pajitnov; the demoscene
Work
Blinkenlights; Tetris; demos
Year
2001; 1984; since the 1980s

In 2001 the Chaos Computer Club turned a Berlin building into an 18 × 8 pixel screen for phone-controlled Pong. Tetris was written in Moscow in 1984 on a computer with no graphics.

Code

hacker culture

A hacker is first of all someone who figures out how a thing works and makes it do the unexpected. Since 2020 the demoscene is on Finland's national inventory of living heritage.
