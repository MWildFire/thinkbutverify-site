Case 02/01. Vermeer ↔ phishing Vermeer ↔ phishingVermeer ↔ phishingVermeer ↔ phishing

Вермеер ↔ фишинг

Art

Artist
Han van Meegeren
Work
The Supper at Emmaus
Year
1937
Where
Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen, Rotterdam

Van Meegeren painted a “Vermeer” and the country's leading expert called it a masterpiece. Chemists later found a 20th-century synthetic resin in the paint.

↔

Code

phishing page

A phishing page copies a bank to the pixel. It is betrayed by what the eye skips: the address and a certificate issued to someone else.

Action

Read the whole address before you type a password.

Перед вводом пароля прочитайте адрес сайта целиком.

The expert believed the fake Vermeer; chemistry did not. A phishing page copies a bank to the pixel, but the address gives it away. Read the whole address before typing a password.
